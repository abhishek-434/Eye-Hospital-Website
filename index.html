Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our treatments, technology, and clinic visit.

LASIK is suitable for adults aged 18 and older with a stable vision prescription for at least 12 months. Before any procedure, we conduct a comprehensive 14-step corneal topography and wavefront diagnostic examination to ensure your corneas are of safe thickness and shape for 100% blade-free laser vision correction.
Cataract surgery is completely painless. We use topical anesthetic eye drops without painful injections. The surgery takes only 15 to 20 minutes with our modern micro-incision phacoemulsification technology, and patients return home the very same day. Most experience clear vision within 24 to 48 hours.

Medically necessary eye surgeries—including robotic cataract surgery, glaucoma valves, retinal laser/vitrectomy, and corneal procedures—are covered by all leading Indian health insurers and TPAs (Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Care Health, Bajaj Allianz, Niva Bupa) as well as CGHS, ECHS, and Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY).
